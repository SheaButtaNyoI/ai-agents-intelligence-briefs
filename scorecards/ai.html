Are you ready for an AI integration?

Ten quick checks for one workflow across up to 2 tools you already pay for. Custom-quoted after a free consult — no checkout price on this page.

1. Can you name one workflow to automate or assist (not "make us AI-powered")?
2. Can you describe today's steps for that workflow end to end?
3. Will this touch no more than 2 tools you already pay for?
4. Does someone on your team own the logins and have authority to grant access?
5. Can you define success in one metric or outcome (time saved, leads captured, fewer missed emails)?
6. Do you know how often it should run (realtime, hourly, daily, on form submit)?
7. Are there compliance or data constraints we must respect (e.g. what data AI may not see)?
8. Is a 14–21 calendar day turnaround workable after quote acceptance, deposit, access, and sample data?
9. Can you do one revision round after the first working version?
10. Is 14 days of email bug-fix support after handoff enough to start (no ongoing ops retainer in the starter)?
