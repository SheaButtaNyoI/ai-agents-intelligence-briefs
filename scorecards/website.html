Are you ready for a new website?

Ten quick checks for a starter site (up to 4 pages). Custom-quoted after a free consult — no checkout price on this page.

1. Can you say in one sentence what the site must do (leads, credibility, bookings, portfolio)?
2. Are up to 4 pages enough for now (home, about, services/offer, contact)?
3. Do you already have (or can get within a week) a logo and brand colors?
4. Do you have draft copy — or clear bullet points we can lightly edit?
5. Do you have photos or are you fine with simple stock/placeholder until you supply them?
6. Do you own or can you buy the domain?
7. Do you have hosting, or are you ready to pick a simple host at handoff?
8. Is there a current site — and if so, do you know what to keep vs. replace?
9. Is a 10–14 calendar day turnaround workable after quote acceptance, deposit, and assets received?
10. Can one person on your side give written feedback in a single revision round?
