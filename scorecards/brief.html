Are you ready for a research brief?

Ten quick checks. You'll get a readiness band and a next step — no obligation.

1. Can you state the question in one or two sentences?
2. Is it one topic (not a multi-workstream project)?
3. Do you have a decision date or a reason you need the answer soon?
4. Do you already know who will use the brief and who decides?
5. Have you shortlisted options, vendors, or answers — or is the landscape still wide open?
6. Would a 5–8 page source-linked PDF actually change the call (vs. needing a full consulting engagement)?
7. Are public primary sources enough (docs, pricing pages, filings, credible reporting) — no need for confidential or paywalled data?
8. Can you name what "good" looks like (recommendation, comparison, risks, costs)?
9. Is there anything that must be out of bounds (competitors, regions, claims to ignore)?
10. If the brief lands in 48 hours after scope confirmation, can your team act on it?
